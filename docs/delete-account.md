# Delete Your MuscleMetria Account

**Last updated: 2026-06-04**

This page explains how MuscleMetria users can request deletion of their account and associated app data.

## Delete Your Account In The App

1. Open MuscleMetria.
2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
3. Go to **Settings**.
4. Open **Account** or **Cloud Sync**.
5. Choose **Delete Account**.
6. Confirm the deletion request.

When you delete your account in the app, MuscleMetria deletes your account and associated cloud data from our production systems.

## What Data Is Deleted

Account deletion removes the following cloud data associated with your account:

- account profile and authentication data
- workout logs and workout execution records
- training plans
- food items, food logs, recipes, nutrition targets, meal plans, and nutrition execution records
- coach-client links, invite records, assigned plans, check-ins, form checks, coach notes, body measurements, health snapshots, progress photos, and related coach media
- app settings, AI entitlement records, AI credit records, and AI usage records associated with your account

Data stored only on your own device is removed if you uninstall the app or clear the app's local data.

## Data We May Keep

Most account data is deleted when your request is processed. We may retain limited records only when required for legal, tax, security, fraud-prevention, abuse-prevention, dispute-resolution, or compliance purposes.

If any such records must be retained, they are kept only for as long as necessary for those purposes and are not used to provide active app features.
