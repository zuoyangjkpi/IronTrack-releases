# MuscleMetria Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-05-30**

## Data Collection

MuscleMetria stores the following data to provide its services:

- **Workout logs**: exercises, sets, reps, weights, dates
- **Training plans**: plan names, day structures, exercises
- **Nutrition data**: food logs, meal plans, recipes, nutrition targets, diet execution status
- **Account information**: email address (if you create an account)
- **Coach-client data**: invite codes, plan and nutrition assignments, check-ins, form checks, progress photos, and planned-vs-actual execution snapshots
- **Optional AI inputs**: text prompts and photos you choose to submit for AI recognition or plan generation

All fitness and nutrition data is stored **locally on your device** by default.

## Cloud Sync

If you choose to sign in, your data is synced to **Supabase** (a secure cloud database). This enables:
- Data backup and recovery
- Coach-client features (sharing plans with your coach)
- Multi-device sync

## Data Sharing

- Your data is **never sold** to third parties
- If you use the coach feature, your assigned coach can view your workout logs, nutrition data, coach-client submissions, and progress information
- You can revoke coach access at any time

## Data Export & Deletion

- You can export all your data at any time from Settings
- You can delete your account and all cloud data from Settings
- You can also review the account deletion steps at: https://zuoyangjkpi.github.io/MuscleMetria-releases/delete-account
- Deleting your account permanently removes your account and app cloud data from our production servers, except data we are required to keep for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution reasons

## Third-Party Services

MuscleMetria uses the following third-party services:
- **Supabase** (database & authentication)
- **OpenFoodFacts** (barcode food lookup)
- **Volcengine Ark / Doubao** (optional AI generation and photo recognition)

AI features are optional and hidden unless enabled. When you use an AI feature, the text or image you submit is sent to the AI provider to generate the requested result.
