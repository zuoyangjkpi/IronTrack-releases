# Delete MuscleMetria Data

**Last updated: 2026-05-30**

This page explains how MuscleMetria users can request deletion of app data without deleting their entire account.

## Delete Data In The App

You can delete many types of MuscleMetria data directly in the app while keeping your account active.

1. Open MuscleMetria.
2. Sign in to your account.
3. Open the area that contains the data you want to delete.
4. Delete the relevant record, plan, entry, photo, coach connection, or local app data.

Depending on the feature, this may include:

- workout logs and workout execution records
- training plans
- food items, food logs, recipes, nutrition targets, meal plans, and nutrition execution records
- coach-client links and assigned plans
- check-ins, form checks, body measurements, progress photos, and related coach media
- app settings and local app data

- the email address associated with your MuscleMetria account
- the type of data you want deleted
- any relevant date range or record description

We may ask for additional information only to verify that you control the account or to identify the data you want deleted.

## What Data Can Be Deleted

You may request deletion of some or all app data associated with your account, including training, nutrition, coach-client, health and fitness, progress photo, form check, and app settings data.

Deleting selected data does not require deleting your MuscleMetria account.

## Data We May Keep

Most requested app data is deleted when your request is processed. We may retain limited records only when required for legal, tax, security, fraud-prevention, abuse-prevention, dispute-resolution, or compliance purposes.

If any such records must be retained, they are kept only for as long as necessary for those purposes and are not used to provide active app features.
